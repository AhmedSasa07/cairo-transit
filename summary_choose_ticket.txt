[5] H4 - Wallet card
Images: []
Text: Wallets are available&nbsp;at all ticket offices and on ticket&nbsp;vending machines in the stations between Haroun and Adly Mansour. Card cost is 80 LE with&nbsp;a minimum top-up of 40 LE&nbsp;on the first purchase. You can charge from 20 pounds to 400 pounds. Anyone can use the card. You can charge the card many times if it is not damaged. Buying a Wallet card saves you time! &nbsp;
------------------------------------------------------------
[6] H4 - Seasonal card
Images: []
Text: The Seasonal card is made for General Public, Students, Elderly (over 60 years olds) or Special Needs. To subscribe you need: Go to the nearest office that offers Seasonal subscriptions on&nbsp; Green Line 3 &nbsp;(Attaba, Abbasia, Heliopolis or Adly Mansour). Provide 2 photographs (format: 4x6). If you are a Student: You need to get the form stamped by your school. A copy of ID or the student's birth certificate should be delivered with the form. A copy of the expenses receipt or a copy of a current or previous school identity card. If you are an Elderly: You need a copy of a valid ID as a proof of age. If you are a Special Need: You need a copy of a valid ID delivered by Governmental Authorities to prove your status.
